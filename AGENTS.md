# Kristina Kidz Academy - Rules and Directives

## 1. Bilingual Support Mandate (Arabic & English)
- The platform **MUST** fully support both **Arabic** (`ar`, RTL) and **English** (`en`, LTR).
- All new features, UI components, modals, products, courses, events, and forms **MUST** provide complete Arabic and English translations.
- Text direction must dynamically toggle between `dir="rtl"` (Arabic) and `dir="ltr"` (English), adapting alignments, chevrons, icons, and typography appropriately.
- The language toggle must remain accessible at all times in the top bar and header navigation, preserving the user's selected language in `localStorage`.

## 2. Odoo 19 Educational Ecosystem Alignment
- Maintain simulation and mapping with Odoo 19 modules:
  - `website`: Academy Landing & Discovery
  - `website_sale`: Courses, Hosting & Care Packages Catalog with Cart & Checkout
  - `website_slides`: Interactive Kids eLearning & Phonics Player
  - `website_event`: Sea Front Activities, Workshops & Booth Booking
  - `hr_attendance`: Attendance & Timetable Management
  - `portal`: Parent Portal & Student Profile
